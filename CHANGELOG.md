# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-15)

First release.

- Merged blader/humanizer v3.1 (workflow, weighting, 26 patterns) with AIScientists-Dev/academic-humanizer (academic and proposal mode), two Indonesian humanizer skills, and selected patterns from stop-slop.
- Added Indonesian equivalents for every general pattern and nine Indonesian-specific patterns (section G).
- Added a measured English vocabulary list computed from the open data of Kobak et al. (2025): 2024 vs 2022 frequency in PubMed abstracts.
- Reversed the blanket passive-voice ban found in other skills, following Reinhart et al. (2025).
- Added review-only and reply modes, author flags (`[perlu sitasi]`, `[perlu bukti]`), and an ethics section on detectors and AI-use disclosure.
- Rewrote all Indonesian examples so that no "after" text contains facts absent from the "before" text.
